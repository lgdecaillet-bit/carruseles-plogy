  • 22 oct 2026, Canva: https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-craft/ (leída en el navegador el 26 oct 2026).
  • "Available now in Canva and to developers through our public API, Craft generates stunning, high-quality images at 2K resolution in any aspect ratio."
  • Arquitectura en dos etapas: un modelo de planificación escribe el plan de la composición y otro lo renderiza "at up to 2K resolution across more than 30 aspect ratios".
  • "Its training data includes millions of human-made templates from Canva's content library".
  • Primer modelo propio: "our first state-of-the-art image generation model"; "the first foundation model built and trained from scratch by Canva Research".
  • Capas: los resultados sirven para Magic Layers, "which can transform generated images into fully editable design elements".
  • Tipos de plantillas: "posters, social posts, presentations, infographics, menus and typography-rich layouts" (lámina 3).
  • "Craft can create text in 55 languages." "Canva Craft is available starting today to all Canva users globally." Dream Lab en el panel inferior izquierdo. Canva MCP: "Coming Soon". Craft-fast y Craft-ultrafast: en camino.
